# Simplistic & Effective Norwegian UI Design Guidelines

## 🇳🇴 Design Philosophy & Benchmarks

1. **Simplistic & Effective Design**:
   - Always keep the UI/UX design **simplistic, functional, and highly effective**.
   - Draw design inspiration and usability standards from leading Scandinavian / Norwegian public transport and marketplace platforms such as **finn.no**, **Skyss**, and **Hudd / Vy**.

2. **Core Norwegian Design Principles**:
   - **Clean White & Crisp Light Palette**: Use clean white backgrounds (`#ffffff`), soft slate surfaces (`#f8fafc`, `#f1f5f9`), and strong high-contrast typography (`#0f172a`, `#1e293b`) for maximum readability in direct outdoor sunlight.
   - **Instant Search & Task Completion**: Prioritize the primary action (e.g., origin/destination selection, search, timetable lookup) at the top with minimal friction, clean input fields, and zero unnecessary visual clutter.
   - **Functional Cards & Contrast**: Use clean borders (`#e2e8f0`), sharp hierarchy, clear badges (e.g., AC/Non-AC, Inter-District), and intuitive icons.
   - **Purposeful Micro-Interactions**: Subtle, responsive hover states and instant tactile feedback without overwhelming animations or heavy background textures.
